Strategy notes specific to a 4-team league
Only 64 roster spots exist total across the league, so this is a shallow league — replacement-level talent is much higher than a standard 10-12 team league. Don't reach to "lock in" a position out of scarcity fear as early as you would in a bigger league; plenty of quality will still be on the board in later rounds.
Prioritize best player available by overall PPR value (using the Top 300 list below) for most of the draft rather than filling need-by-need, since in a 4-team league talent depth at RB/WR runs deep and there's little risk of a position "drying up" before your next pick.
QB and TE are where the shallow format matters least — with only 4 teams, elite QB/TE separation from replacement level is smaller relative to a deep league, so it's fine to wait on both unless a top-tier one falls unusually far.
Once starting lineup needs are filled, shift priority to: backup QB → backup TE → best remaining RB/WR for bench depth.
Kicker and D/ST: draft these last, only after the full starting lineup (minus maybe FLEX) and bench targets above are secured — never reach for K/DST early.

Trade rule of thumb
For every player in the trade, find their overall rank in the PPR Top 300 list below and convert it to a value score: value = 301 − rank (rank 1 = 300 points, rank 100 = 201 points; a player not on the list = 0 points).
Sum the value of the players I'd receive, and sum the value of the players I'd send.
Accept if received value ≥ sent value × 0.90 (I'm losing no more than 10% of value). This small tolerance exists because in a 4-team league, fixing a real roster hole is worth a modest value discount.
Reject if received value < sent value × 0.90.
Rule 5 overrides 3/4 — auto-reject regardless of value math if completing the trade would leave me unable to fill a full starting lineup (1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 1 FLEX, 1 D/ST, 1 K) from my remaining roster.
Rule 6 overrides 3/4 — auto-reject if the trade would leave me with zero players at QB or TE (no starter, no backup).
Rule 7 overrides 3/4 — auto-accept if the trade fills a starting-lineup spot I currently have zero eligible players for, and received value ≥ sent value × 0.75. An unfillable starting slot is a bigger problem than a moderate value loss in a 4-team league.
